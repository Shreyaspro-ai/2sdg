## Verification and remaining differences

All eleven templates pass title, local-asset and internal-route checks. Local navigation and project review were exercised in the browser. Reference-sized browser captures were inspected during the repair. The four uploaded motion files are byte-for-byte unchanged.

This is a closer reconstruction, not certified pixel equivalence. The references contain raster-generated lettering without original font files, and some city artwork contains annotations or partial overlays. Native labels cover those regions, but some edge/fringe discrepancies and uncovered sky transitions remain. The handwritten simulator phrase, icon details in secondary cards, and exact optical font metrics still differ. Source photographs for areas hidden by original panels were not supplied. No unseen Welcome page or backend simulation was invented.
